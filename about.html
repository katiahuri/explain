Katia Stainfeld Huri spent almost 20 years building software and neural networks. ExplAIn teaches the part she kept seeing engineers miss: the system between the model and the product.

Katia Stainfeld Huri, founder and instructor

Why I built this course

I trained, taught, patented and optimized neural networks, down to making ResNet and BERT inference run faster.

Almost 20 years of it, across the whole stack: frontend, backend, data engineering, data science, low-level systems. Three patents. A startup acquired for its core team. Teams of data analysts, engineers and scientists.

I have seen engineering from every side. And the same thing kept happening: strong engineers using AI every day, and still guessing. Not because they couldn’t understand it, but because nobody ever showed them the layer beneath the interface.

So that’s what you get here. You’ll look at any AI product and explain how it actually works. You’ll know why it hallucinated, whether you need retrieval or fine-tuning, what belongs in a prompt and what belongs in code.

I won’t teach you prompts. Prompts expire. I’ll teach you the system underneath, and that doesn’t.
