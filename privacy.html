No cookies, and no third-party analytics

This site sets no cookies. The only thing stored in your browser is a single sessionStorage entry that marks one visit, and your browser discards it when you close the tab.

There is no Google Analytics here, no advertising pixel, no social tracker and no session recorder. The traffic counting is our own code writing to our own database.

The visitor code is deliberately weak as an identifier: it is a hash of your address, your browser and the current date, so it changes every day and cannot follow you across weeks.

What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your details with anyone for their own use.
  • We do not build advertising profiles.
  • We do not store raw IP addresses.

Payments

When the course opens, payments will be handled by [PAYMENT PROVIDER], acting as the merchant of record. They take your payment details on their own systems and under their own privacy policy. Card numbers never reach this site and we never see them. We receive the fact of a purchase, the product bought, and the email address to deliver it to.

Who else touches the data

  • Vercel — hosting. Serving a page means their systems process the request.
  • Neon — the Postgres database holding sign-ups and traffic counts.
  • Google Fonts — the typeface is loaded from Google's servers, so your browser's request reaches Google, including your IP address. We use no other Google service.
  • [PAYMENT PROVIDER] — once payments are live, as described above.
  • [EMAIL PROVIDER] — to send the emails you asked for.

If you are in the UK or the EU, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date at the top changes with it. Anyone on the list will be told by email before a change takes effect.
