Machines that learn · 9 min

The first neural era

  1. 1943 · A neuron as logic

    McCulloch and Pitts write a brain cell as mathematics.

  2. 1956 · The field is named

    The Dartmouth workshop coins “artificial intelligence”.

  3. 1958 · It learns from data

    Rosenblatt’s perceptron adjusts its own weights.

  4. 1969 · The limit is proved

    Minsky and Papert show a single layer cannot do XOR.

Two winters · 9 min

Then the money left. Twice.

01

1974–1980

The Lighthill report judged AI had not delivered, and British funding was cut.

02

1987–1993

The Lisp machine market collapsed as ordinary workstations got cheaper.

03

What survived both

Backpropagation, published 1970 and popularised in 1986. LSTM in 1997.

Two winters

The ideas were right. The computers were not.

Both collapses followed promises sized for hardware that did not exist.

What actually changed · 11 min

None of it was new maths

01

Data · 2009

ImageNet: 1.2 million labelled images, far more than anyone had trained on.

02

Compute · 2012

AlexNet on two GPUs: 15.3% top-five error against 26.2% for second place.

03

Architecture · 2017

The transformer dropped recurrence, so training could run in parallel.

04

Scale · 2020 on

Scaling laws showed loss falls predictably as model, data and compute grow.

What actually changed

What was old. What was new.

Old, and still running

17th to 19th century mathematics.

  • Chain rule, 1676
  • Least squares, 1805
  • Gradient descent, 1847
  • Markov chains, 1906

New in the 2010s

None of it a new branch of maths.

  • The internet as a training set
  • GPUs doing the arithmetic
  • One architecture: 2017
  • Money at industrial scale

The payoff

Compute, data, architecture.

Nothing on the mathematics side had to be invented.

Five myths to leave at the door

MythWhat is actually true
“AI was invented in the last few years.”Most of its maths is 17th to 19th century.
“Neural networks are a new idea.”Rosenblatt built a learning machine in 1958.
“Hinton invented backpropagation.”Linnainmaa published it in 1970; 1986 spread it.
“AI progress has been steady.”Funding collapsed twice: 1974 and 1987.
“The transformer was a maths leap.”It made training parallel. Scale did the rest.

Check your history

A vendor calls their method a brand-new breakthrough.

Name two questions that test the claim.

Show the answer
  • Which part is actually new: the mathematics, the architecture, the data or the compute?
  • What is the oldest idea it depends on, and what year is that idea from?
  • If it is old mathematics at new scale, what changed to make it work now?
  • Has this been tried before and abandoned, and what killed it then?
  • A good answer names the layer the novelty sits in. Almost always it is data, compute or packaging.
