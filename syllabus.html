The gap

You use AI.
But do you understand it?

Most AI education teaches the interface: which tool to click, which prompt to copy, which feature launched this week. ExplAIn teaches the system underneath—knowledge that stays useful when the tools change.

Is it for you?

Technical depth.
Human language.

ExplAIn sits between superficial tool tutorials and academic machine-learning courses. It’s designed for people who need a strong conceptual foundation they can actually use.

This course is for you if…

  • You’re a software engineer, data professional, technical PM, founder or engineering leader.
  • You already use AI, but the concepts still feel fragmented or overly abstract.
  • You want to design, evaluate or lead AI work—not just operate a chat interface.
  • You prefer durable understanding over weekly lists of “the hottest tools.”

It’s not built for…

  • People looking only for ready-made prompts.
  • A full mathematical derivation of transformer architecture.
  • A certification based on passive watching.
  • A course tied to one model, vendor or interface.
