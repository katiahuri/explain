The transformation

From AI builder to AI owner

Builders make AI work. Owners know why it works, and where it breaks.

Builder

  • Swaps the model when an answer comes back wrong
  • Rewrites the prompt and hopes it holds
  • Calls the whole product “the AI”
  • Ships on a demo that worked once

→

Owner

  • Names the part that failed, then fixes that part
  • Decides what enters the context, and in what order
  • Knows where the model ends and the system begins
  • Ships on a set of real cases that has to keep passing
